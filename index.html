Tier 1 Exam Pattern Rules:

  • Duration: 60 minutes for 100 questions.
  • Marking Scheme: +2 marks for correct answer, -0.50 negative marking for every incorrect answer.
  • Key to Clearance: High accuracy in Maths and Reasoning, swift solving in English and GK.

⭐ 5 Golden Preparation Rules

  • 1. Previous Year Questions (PYQs) Daily: SSC repeats question types regularly. Dedicate 6:15–7:00 PM strictly to PYQs from the last 5 years.
  • 2. Dedicated Revision on Days 7, 14, 21: Do not move forward without consolidating the previous 6 days. Take full sectional tests on revision days.
  • 3. Formula & Rules Notebook: Keep a pocket notebook for Maths identities, English idioms/grammar rules, and important GK dates/articles.
  • 4. Systematic Error Analysis: Spend 30 minutes every evening analyzing why a question was wrong. Never ignore a silly mistake.
  • 5. Final Week Mock Sprint (Days 22–28): Shift emphasis to full-length timed tests under exam pressure to master time allocation across the 4 sections.
